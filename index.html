WELCOME TO GOLDEN DRAGON CASINO
Welcome to 5 Nights in the Forest
Survive the nights. Free your friends. Escape the forest.

Goal

Two friends were dragged off and caged in separate camps deep in the forest — one guarded by 5 monsters, the other by 10. Kill every guard at a camp, then walk up and press E to free the friend there — they'll wait safe back at your campfire. Free both, find the key hidden in the monster's den, and bring it all back to the exit door to escape together.

How to play

  • WASD — move around · SHIFT — sprint
  • E — chop trees for wood, open doors, fix the anvil
  • R — spear animals (health) or monsters (bones) · G — shoot with the gun (uses ammo)
  • X — target: long-range aimed shot at the nearest animal/monster, even far away (needs gun + ammo)
  • F — feed the campfire with wood to keep it burning
  • P — plant a sapling · 1 / 2 — craft gun / ammo
  • 3 / 4 — upgrade your axe / spear at the anvil · 5 / 6 — upgrade Friend 1 / Friend 2
  • T — switch the torch on/off · 7 — build a torch battery at the anvil (15 Scrap, 5 Bones)
  • V — change camera view · M — open the map · Z — pause
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen joystick and buttons.

Stay alive

Your fire keeps a safe boundary around you — let it run out and the dark gets dangerous. At night, monsters roam and a giant may awaken, so keep an eye on your health, fire, and stamina bars in the top corner. Every 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th... night is a War Night — a tough zombie appears, so be ready to fight or help a freed friend take it down. Build a torch battery at the anvil and switch on the torch (T) when things get crowded — every monster except the giant will bolt away from the light. One battery only holds 30 seconds of charge, then it's spent, so use it wisely.
